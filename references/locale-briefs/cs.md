## Audience and register

- A friendly coach in everyday Czech: short sentences, one idea per line, the ordinary word rather than the exact rare one. A reader who knows no English must understand each line on first read, because the copy sits beside a timer and a money figure.
- The app speaks about itself in the first person plural in 19 `en` units ("We couldn't load the data.", `component.errorView.message`; "We covered %@ with a streak freeze.", `streakFreeze.usedBody`). Czech carries this either as a 1pl verb ("Nepodařilo se nám načíst data.") or, more usually in system-register copy, impersonally ("Data se nepodařilo načíst."). ## Address and gender

- Formality is an open decision (`address_form`), because the two candidate registers are both evidenced and the choice is the owner's.
  - Apple's iOS Czech addresses the user with **vy**: "Aplikace „%@“ vám chce posílat oznámení" (`System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/UserNotificationsServer.framework | Localizable.strings | USER_NOTIFICATION_PERMISSION_ALERT_TITLE`), "Když aplikaci %@ povolíte přístup … údajů o vaší aktivitě" (`Applications/FamilyControlsAuthenticationUI.app | Localizable.strings | NOTIFICATION_AUTH_REQUEST_MESSAGE_INDIVIDUAL`), "Kategorie zdravotních dat můžete zapnout později" (`HealthUI.framework | HealthUI-Localizable.strings | AUTHORIZATION_DONT_ALLOW_ALERT_MESSAGE`). The l10n.cz Stylistická příručka (fetched 2026-09-16, https://l10n.cz/wiki/Slovníky/Stylistická_příručka/) states it as the rule: "Pro oslovení uživatele používejte vykání („zadejte“, nikoliv „zadej“)."
  - Apple's own coaching copy in Fitness and Activity uses **ty**: "Uzavři svoje kroužky" (`System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/ActivityUI.framework | Localizable.strings | THOPHY_CASE_ACTIVITY`), "Tohle ocenění tě čeká, když zvládneš … sérii pohybové aktivity. To dáš!" (`System/Library/Health/Assets/FitnessUIAssets.bundle/ActivityAchievements/MonthlyAchievements/localization/challenge/MoveStreak_SMALL_INCREASE | Localizable-Modified-tinker.stringsdict | ACHIEVEMENT_UNACHIEVED_ALERT_DESCRIPTION`), "Toto ocenění máš za spálení nejvíc kalorií" (`FitnessUIAssets.bundle/ActivityAchievements/BestWorkout-HKWorkoutActivityTypeRowing/localization | Localizable.stringsdict | ACHIEVEMENT_ACHIEVED_DESCRIPTION_CAL`). That is the same genre as Moneyfesting: streaks, badges and a daily ring.
- Whatever is decided, buttons do not change: the Czech control form is the infinitive, which carries no person at all (Control forms). The decision touches finite verbs in running text, possessives and the one control role `Retry.in_sentence`.
- Gender. Czech forces the reader's gender in the past tense in both registers: `ty` gives "byl jsi / byla jsi", `vy` gives "byli jste / byly jste". Gender is therefore not a tiebreaker between the two, and the neutral strategies below apply either way.
  - Present tense instead of past. "You showed up %lld days in a row." (`achievements.streak.body`) as a count plus a nominal second sentence, not a past-tense verb about the reader.
  - Verbless, nominal sentences. "Sedm dní, ani jedna mezera." for "Seven days, no gaps." (`achievements.badge.hired.earned`); "Kruh uzavřen." for "Ring closed." (`achievements.badge.fullDayFirst.earned`) — the participle agrees with "kruh", not with the reader.
  - The impersonal neuter `-o` form where no noun is visible: "Pokryto zmrazením." for "Covered by a freeze." (`widgets.streak.copy.frozen.small`).
  - Agreement with the counted object, never with the reader: "%lld aplikace zablokovaná / %lld aplikace zablokované / %lld aplikací zablokovaných" (`screenTime.restrictedApps.appsRestricted`), "%lld vybraná / %lld vybrané / %lld vybraných" (`themePicker.manageThemes.selectedCount`).
  - Infinitives and verbal nouns in headers and criteria: "Soustředit se dva dny po sobě." for `achievements.badge.dayTwo.criterion`.
  - Role nouns are the hard case. `en` hotspots: `achievements.badge.hired.name` "Hired", `.salaried.name` "Salaried", `.rehired.name` "Rehired", `.permanent.name` "Permanent", `.tenure.name` "Tenure", `.clockedIn.name` "Clocked in", `.backOnShift.name` "Back on shift". Czech has no gender-neutral personal noun for these, so the strategy is to name the event, not the person: "Nástup", "Návrat", "Na výplatě", "Na dobu neurčitou", "Odpracované roky", "Příchod", "Zpátky na směně". This is the badge-conceit open decision.
  - Where no neutral form exists at all, the shipped default is the masculine, recorded per key. At snapshot exactly one `en` key needs it: `user.gender.other` "Other" -> "Jiné" (neuter, and therefore neutral); the badge names above avoid the problem entirely under the event-noun strategy, so there is no masculine default to record until a batch proves one necessary.
  - `achievements.badge.fullDaysFifty.earned` "Fifty full days. A whole season of showing up." is the other hotspot: the plain rendering carries no participle about the reader ("Padesát celých dnů. Celá sezóna bez vynechání.").
- Grep aid, not a validator: `jsi | jste | tvůj | tvoje | tvá | váš | vaše | \bl jsi\b | \bla jsi\b | \bli jste\b` (the last three catch a verb ending in "-l"/"-la"/"-li" followed by "jsi"/"jste", e.g. "byl jsi", "byla jsi", "udělali jste" — a plain-word grep for "jsi"/"jste" already catches the same lines). A hit is a line to read, not a defect.
- The stored profile gender (`Manifesting/Features/User/Domain/Entities/Gender.swift`) is not a copy input without a product decision.

## Header and label forms

- Sentence case everywhere in app-owned copy: only the first word of a heading takes a capital, plus proper names. l10n.cz Stylistická příručka (fetched 2026-09-16): "S velkým počátečním písmenem pište jen první slovo nadpisu." Apple's Czech values in Platform terms are sentence case throughout ("Upravit plochu", "Přidat widget", "Spravovat předplacené služby"). The 114 Title Case `en` keys therefore lose their inner capitals: "Edit Focus Task" (`focusTask.createTask.screenTitleEdit`) -> "Upravit úkol soustředění", "My Tasks" (`focusTask.picker.myTasksHeader`) -> "Moje úkoly".
- Proper names keep their case inside a sentence: Moneyfesting, Nastavení, Plocha, Fotky, Zdraví, Zprávy, Čas u obrazovky, Face ID, App Store, Stav mysli.
- Uppercase eyebrows and stat captions stay uppercase when `en` is (34 all-caps `en` strings), and Czech uppercasing is safe: it preserves every diacritic and changes no letter's identity. `String.uppercased(with: Locale(identifier: "cs_CZ"))` on Foundation, macOS Version 15.7.9 (Build 24G830): "Nálada" -> "NÁLADA", "Hodnota času, 30 dní" -> "HODNOTA ČASU, 30 DNÍ", "důležité" -> "DŮLEŽITÉ", "chuť" -> "CHUŤ", "čeština" -> "ČEŠTINA". Czech has no dotted/dotless `i` pair (Turkish), no `ß` (German) and no final sigma (Greek), so an uppercase transform never changes the letter count or the spelling. Apple's own Czech all-caps keeps accents: en "GOAL" -> cs "CÍL" (`System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/HealthToolbox.framework \| WellnessDashboard-Localizable.strings \| SLEEP_GOAL_HEADER`), en "FREE TRIAL" -> cs "ZKUSIT ZDARMA" (`System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/AppStoreKit.framework \| Localizable.strings \| INTRO_PRICE_OFFER_FREE_TRIAL`). Seed forms: MOOD -> NÁLADA, DAY STREAK -> DENNÍ SÉRIE, TASKS DONE -> HOTOVÉ ÚKOLY, TODAY'S FOCUS -> DNEŠNÍ SOUSTŘEDĚNÍ, TIME VALUE, 30 DAYS -> HODNOTA ČASU, 30 DNÍ, LAST 30 DAYS -> POSLEDNÍCH 30 DNÍ, EST. TIME VALUE -> ODHADOVANÁ HODNOTA ČASU (no period: "Est." truncates a word in English, but "odhad"/"odhadovaná" truncates nothing in Czech, so carrying the source's period over would read as two clipped sentences). These expand hardest (Length trigger).
- Section headers are nouns or impersonal neuter participles agreeing with the listed items, never with the reader: `plan.dailyPlan.plannedHeader` PLANNED -> NAPLÁNOVÁNO, `.doneHeader` DONE -> DOKONČENO, `.anytimeHeader` ANYTIME -> KDYKOLI. Sentence-case headers take a plain noun: "Recently earned" (`achievements.gallery.recentSection`) -> "Nedávno získané" (agreeing with "odznaky").
- Tab titles are nouns: Moneyfesting, Plán, Přehledy, Profil (`main.tab.*`). "Insights" -> "Přehledy" is Apple's own Czech (`System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/AccessibilitySharedUISupport.framework \| AccessibilitySharedUISupport.strings \| Insights`).
- Toggles and state labels take a short adjective or participle agreeing with the labelled noun, in the plural form the count selects: "%lld aplikace zablokovaná / %lld aplikace zablokované / %lld aplikací zablokovaných" (`screenTime.restrictedApps.appsRestricted`); "Soustředění pozastaveno" (`widgets.startFocus.paused`, neuter, agreeing with "soustředění"); "Probíhá" (`widgets.startFocus.inProgress`).
- A fragment with no visible noun agrees with the noun the screen implies, and where that noun is not certain the impersonal neuter `-o` form is the safe default: `widgets.streak.copy.frozen.small` "Covered by a freeze." -> "Pokryto zmrazením." beside the medium line, which does have a noun ("Včerejšek je pokrytý."). The packet's `agreement_target` names the noun; a mismatch between the two sizes is the fault the editor checks.
- Status pills that describe the action rather than the user take the impersonal neuter: "Uloženo" (`common.editor.saved`).

## Short forms and units

## Numbers, dates, currency and punctuation

All Foundation and ICU values below are `cs_CZ` output recorded in the harvested `locale-formats.json` (ICU 76.1.0.0, CLDR 46.0.0.0); invisible characters are named by code point.

- Runtime values arrive formatted through `AppLanguage.effectiveLocale` (app `CLAUDE.md:42`), so copy never hard-codes a number or a currency. Never write "Kč" or "koruna" into a string: the currency is the user's own choice and independent of the language.
- Decimal comma, thousands separator U+00A0, and four-digit numbers are grouped: 2 000, 12 000, 1 250,5, 1 250 000.
- Money through the app's own `AppCurrency.format`: "120" + U+00A0 + "CZK" and "1" + U+00A0 + "250,5" + U+00A0 + "CZK" (ISO code after the amount, because CZK is not one of the four narrow-symbol codes). The locale's own standard form would be "120,00" + U+00A0 + "Kč". Compact figures are the engineering flag in Identity.
- Dates: "31. 12. 2026" with U+202F (narrow no-break space) after each period, "31. prosince 2026" spelled, weekday "úterý", weekday plus date "úterý 12. 5." with the same U+202F. Month and weekday names are lower case. The ascending day-month-year order with a space after each ordinal period is the ÚJČ rule (Internetová jazyková příručka, "Kalendářní datum a místo původu", https://prirucka.ujc.cas.cz/?id=810, fetched 2026-09-16).
- Time is 24-hour (`Locale.hourCycle` h23, `DateFormatter` template `j` resolves to `H`): "9:41", "23:59". The `en` axis labels 12AM / 06AM / 12PM / 06PM (`insights.appUsage.axis*`, `profile.appUsage.axis.*`, `screenTimeReport.axis*`, 12 keys) become 0:00 / 6:00 / 12:00 / 18:00.
- Ranges take an en dash with no spaces around single-word members ("1–3 hodiny", "18–24", "5–120 minut"); the ÚJČ page above gives the dash rule and the spacing distinction for multi-word members. Percent is separated: "56 %", "100 %" with U+00A0 before the sign. Multiplication uses × ("Hodiny soustředění × hodinová sazba", `achievements.category.wages.description`; possessive dropped per the Audience-and-register rule, the owner being obvious).
- Quotation marks are „ “ (U+201E, U+201C), nested ‚ ‘ (U+201A, U+2018). Apple's Czech uses them for the app name inside its own alerts: "Aplikace „%@“ vám chce posílat oznámení". The 11 `en` keys with straight double quotes all convert. Attribution in the notification extension takes an en dash plus the name; the ellipsis rule is under Decided conflicts.
- Apple's Czech puts U+00A0 after one-letter prepositions and conjunctions (k, u, o, a, v, s, z): "údajů o" + U+00A0 + "vaší aktivitě", "aplikací a" + U+00A0 + "webových stránek" (`Applications/FamilyControlsAuthenticationUI.app | Localizable.strings | NOTIFICATION_AUTH_REQUEST_MESSAGE_INDIVIDUAL`), "Můžete je nakonfigurovat v" + U+00A0 + "Nastavení." (`UserNotificationsServer.framework | Localizable.strings | USER_NOTIFICATION_PERMISSION_ALERT_BODY`). It reaches inside the feature name itself: Apple's Screen Time is "Čas u" + U+00A0 + "obrazovky" (`Applications/Preferences.app | Localizable.strings | Screen Time`), so a plain space there is a mismatch with the device, not a typographic nicety. Reproduce it exactly in every mirrored string; in app-owned copy it is preferred and checked at render time, because a missing one is invisible until a line wraps badly.
- Numerals attach to nouns by case, and the plural category is the mechanism. Evidence, all iOS 26.1 (23B86) runtime, `cs.lproj`:
  - days, `System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/CalendarUIKit.framework | Localizable.stringsdict | repeat_interval_days`: one "%lu den", few "%lu dny", many "%lu dne", other "%lu dní" (the same four in `System/Library/Health/FeedItemPlugins/MenstrualCyclesAppPlugin.healthplugin | Localizable.stringsdict | PREGNANCY_DAYS_UNIT`, with U+00A0 before the noun).
  - minutes and hours, `System/Library/CoreServices/VoiceOverTouch.app | VOTLocalizedStrings.stringsdict | hours.minutes.format`: one "%d minuta", few "%d minuty", many "%d minuty", other "%d minut"; one "%d hodina", few "%d hodiny", many "%d hodiny", other "%d hodin".
  - apps, `System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/ScreenTimeSettingsUI.framework | Localizable.stringsdict | NumberOfAppsFormat`: one "%lu aplikace", few "%lu aplikace", many "%lu aplikace", other "%lu aplikací".
  So: `one` = nominative singular, `few` = nominative plural, `other` = genitive plural, `many` = genitive singular. Never reuse one branch for another; a count label written with a single form is wrong at three of the four boundaries.
- Plural categories are one / few / many / other. Which integers reach which (ICU 76.1.0.0 probe over 0–999999, confirmed by a compiled-catalog runtime check at 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 12, 20, 22, 100, 101, 1000000, 1000001, 2000000): `one` is the single integer 1; `few` is 2, 3, 4 and nothing else; `other` is everything else including 0, 5, 12, 20, 21, 22, 100, 101, 120, 1000 and every larger integer; `many` is reached by **no integer at all** — only fractions select it (0.5, 1.5, 2.5 all return `many`). Exercise 0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 12, 21, 101 and 1000000; the surprises worth a screenshot are 2 (few) against 12 (other), and 1 (one) against 101 (other).
- Write the `many` branch anyway. Xcode 26.1 lists one, few, many, other for `cs` (`/Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/SharedFrameworks/XCStringsParser.framework/Versions/A/Resources/variationConfigFile.plist`, `PluralRule.ComplementedPluralKeys.cs`), so the catalog asks for it, and Apple supplies it in every stringsdict above. With a `%lld` count it never renders, so it costs a line and risks nothing. The one place a fraction could reach the user is `widgets.cash.focusedHoursDecimal` "%@ focused hours", whose value is a pre-formatted `%@` string: a plural variation cannot key off it, so the Czech noun there has to work for "3,5" — flag it as a source constraint, do not bend the number.
- Six `en` keys carry a single form where the count plainly varies and `ro` already splits them (`achievements.streak.body`, `.comebackBody`, `.headline`, `.milestoneBody`, `.nextMilestoneBody`, `.weekAccessibility`); Czech needs the same split, plus the 29 keys that already have `en` variations.

## Length trigger

- A `cs` string longer than **1.25×** its `en` length goes on the render list (`scripts/catalog_check.py` C5); it is never a finding by itself. The ratio is measured from Apple's own translations, not guessed: across 78 029 English/Czech pairs from the iOS 26.1 (23B86) runtime (45 758 unique, VoiceOver bundles and accessibility tables excluded), 27.9% exceed 1.25× — the same share of Apple's Czech that `ro`'s 1.35× catches of Apple's Romanian, which is the trigger calibrated against the 299-of-1135 `ro` catalog figure. A flat 1.35× would flag only 19.6% of Czech and let real expansion through.
- Short strings expand most: for `en` of 1–10 characters the median is 1.20× and the 90th percentile 2.00×; at 41–120 characters the median is 1.05×. Uppercase is the worst case — of 919 all-caps pairs, 31.9% exceed 1.35× and the 90th percentile is 2.00×, so the eyebrows and stat captions of Header and label forms are where a render is not optional.
- There is no `cs` catalog share to quote at this snapshot: 0 `cs` units exist. Recompute the share against the real drafts after the first batch and replace this bullet.
- Counts never establish fit. Verdicts come from render evidence against each view's own `minimumScaleFactor` and `lineLimit` (`references/surface-budgets.md`, `references/render-evidence.md`). Shortening cuts words, never facts; accessibility strings are exempt.

## Open decisions

- **Address form** (`address_form`). Affects every finite verb and possessive in app-owned copy — the 177 `en` units with a second-person possessive, the reader-predicate keys in the `en` inventory, the idiom proposals `onboarding.widgets.howTo.placeTitle` ("Umísti si ho, kam chceš.") and `moneyfesting.details.stopConfirmationMessage` ("vrať se s čistou hlavou"), and the control role `Retry.in_sentence`. Rule: app-owned copy uses one address throughout, while strings that mirror an iOS label or alert follow the device regardless (Platform terms). Options: **vy**, with 2pl verbs and "vaše" (0 keys today; evidenced by Apple's iOS Czech system copy and the l10n.cz Stylistická příručka, https://l10n.cz/wiki/Slovníky/Stylistická_příručka/ fetched 2026-09-16: "Pro oslovení uživatele používejte vykání"); **ty**, with 2sg verbs and "tvoje" (0 keys; evidenced by Apple's own Czech Fitness and Activity coaching copy, "Uzavři svoje kroužky" and "To dáš!", and by the app's Romanian precedent of informal `tu`). Recommended: **vy**, because every string this app is required to mirror verbatim — the two mock alerts and the nine how-to labels — is already `vy`, so one register covers the whole widgets how-to screen an editor reads in one pass. Meanwhile: agents write in forms that carry no person wherever the meaning allows (infinitive buttons, nominal headers, impersonal neuter states, present tense with the pronoun dropped), and use `vy` where a finite second-person verb is unavoidable, so a flip to `ty` touches a countable list the adjudicator reports rather than the whole batch.
- **Focus term** (`focus_session`). Affects `moneyfesting.details.*`, `focusTask.*`, `widgets.startFocus.*`, `widgets.focusRing.*`, `profile.home.focusSection`, `onboarding.dailyFocusGoal.*` and every `a11y` label naming a session. Rule: one word per concept across the catalog; a flip is a global term change with its own follow-up batch, never a per-string choice. Options: **soustředění** (0 keys; plain Czech that a reader without English understands, and the word Apple uses for the system Focus mode, `Applications/Preferences.app | Localizable.strings | Focus`); **fokus** (0 keys; an English loan that reads as jargon and gives no natural adjective). Recommended: **soustředění**, because it is the everyday word and the overlap with Apple's Focus mode is exactly the overlap `en` already accepts by calling both things "Focus". Meanwhile: the first batch writes "soustředění" and the adjudicator lists every key that touched the term; the overlap to weigh is the bare label `profile.home.focusSection` ("Soustředění"), which is also the name of an iOS Settings row.
- **Streak freeze image** (`streak_freeze`). Affects the 12 keys under `streakFreeze.*`, `paywall.benefit.streak.*` and `widgets.streak.copy.frozen.*`. Rule: where no local idiom carries the source image, state the meaning plainly rather than translating the picture, and never strengthen the promise (one earned freeze covers one day, `paywall.benefit.streak.detail`). Options: **zmrazení série** (0 keys; keeps the source image, and "zmrazit" is the ordinary Czech verb for freezing an account or a price, so the metaphor lands); **pojistka série** (0 keys; more idiomatic Czech for a safeguard, but "pojistka" promises insurance-like protection the code does not provide and invites "unlimited" readings). Recommended: **zmrazení série**, because it keeps the source promise exactly and the plainer word is the one the disclaimer-heavy money frame can live beside. Meanwhile: the first batch uses "zmrazení série" and flags every line where it reads awkwardly, so the owner decides on real sentences rather than on the term alone.
- **Badge workplace conceit** (`badge_conceit`). Affects the 24 `achievements.badge.*.name` keys, the 5 `achievements.category.*.name` keys and the `earned` lines in `BadgeCopy+{Comeback,FullDays,Payroll,Shift,Wages}.swift`. Rule: the conceit is decided once per locale and every badge follows it; unlock criteria stay literal. Options: **keep the conceit with Czech workplace event nouns** ("Příchod", "Nástup", "Na výplatě", "Návrat", "Na dobu neurčitou", "Odpracované roky", "Celá směna", "Dvojitá směna", "Směna", "Výplaty"; 0 keys, and the event-noun form is what keeps the names gender-neutral, Address and gender — "Na výplatě" replaces an earlier "Výplatní páska", a physical payslip that named an object rather than an event like every sibling badge); **drop the conceit for plain achievement nouns** ("První sezení", "Týden v kuse", "Dvacet ze třiceti"; 0 keys, neutral by construction but it loses the reason the app prices time like work). Recommended: **keep the conceit**, because the workplace frame is what makes "your hours have a price" legible without ever saying the app pays anyone, and the Czech shift-and-payroll vocabulary is everyday speech. Meanwhile: the first badge batch writes the event nouns listed in Idioms and figurative language, marks each as a proposal, and the adjudicator lists every badge key that touched the conceit so a flip stays one scope.
