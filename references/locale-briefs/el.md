## Identity

- Tag `el`, bare code, no region. `el-GR` negotiates to `el` in both the v1 and wave-2 preference lists, iOS ships `el.lproj` (24 of 26 UI bundles checked; absent only from `UIKit.framework` and `ScreenTime.framework`, whose strings the app reaches through `UIKitCore.framework`, which has it), and `xcstringstool compile` writes `el.lproj` (`LOCLocaleCode.plist`: displayName "Greek", lproj "el"). Source: `apple-coverage.json` `locale.recommended_catalog_tag`, `.tag_reason`, `.ios_lproj_presence`, `.system_negotiation`.
- Audience: Greek speakers in Greece and Cyprus reading an iPhone in Greek. Greek script, left to right (`locale-formats.json` `el.direction` = ltr). Format locale `el_GR`; audience currency EUR.
- Snapshot: 1187 keys at `c8a49dd`, 1189 in the working tree, where `en` is localized on 1142 units and `ro` on 1135; **`el` on 0**. No `el` unit exists to count, compare or lock.
- Precedence: the app `CLAUDE.md`, then **Apple's Greek wording** for anything that mirrors or names an iOS surface (Platform terms below), then this brief. `glossary/el.json` records the outcomes.
- Provenance: every row is agent research. There is no shipped Greek copy and no native source. A native read of the hero set, the shield pairs and the store text is **recommended before the pilot batch**, and stays recommended on the first ship (SKILL.md `## Verify and deliver`); "seed 2026-09-16" marks a row the owner may flip, `decided_by: "skill-seed-2026-09-16"` marks the same in the glossary.
- Tone examples: none yet. The first batch returns six rows in the `references/examples-ro.md` format, which the coordinator saves as `references/examples-el.md`; until then the translator gets `examples-ro.md` as pattern illustration only. Budgets: `references/surface-budgets.md`.

## Audience and register

The reader is a Greek iPhone user who wants fewer hours on the phone and is being shown what those hours are worth at a rate they set themselves. Talk like a calm local coach: everyday Greek, one idea per line, short sentences, the ordinary word over the learned one, no English sentence shapes and no bureaucratic Greek. A reader who knows no English must understand each line at first read; a reader who knows Greek officialese must not feel addressed by a form.

Imported-English shapes the editor flags. The first two columns are the marker list the coordinator appends to the editor prompt, **target text only**; the key column stays coordinator-side (`references/agent-protocol.md` `## Packet separation`). Every Greek example is an **agent-authored illustration**, not shipped copy: no Greek unit exists yet.

## Address and gender

- **Formality is an owner decision** (`address_form`, Open decisions). Until it closes, app-owned copy uses **εσύ** (second person singular) and strings that mirror an iOS label or alert use **εσείς**, because the device does.
- Apple's register, harvested 2026-09-16: address `εσείς` (`σας`, `-ετε`); buttons are `να`-clauses or verbal nouns, not imperatives; sentence case (`platform-terms.json` `register_summary.el`). Evidence: "Αν παραχωρήσετε στην εφαρμογή «%@» το δικαίωμα πρόσβασης στον Χρόνο επί οθόνης…" (iOS 26.1 (23B86) runtime: `Applications/FamilyControlsAuthenticationUI.app` | `Localizable.strings` | `NOTIFICATION_AUTH_REQUEST_MESSAGE_INDIVIDUAL`); "Μπορείτε να ενεργοποιήσετε κατηγορίες δεδομένων Υγείας αργότερα…" (`System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/HealthUI.framework` | `HealthUI-Localizable.strings` | `AUTHORIZATION_DONT_ALLOW_ALERT_MESSAGE`).
- Apple itself drops to **εσύ** when it coaches: "Για να κερδίσεις αυτό το βραβείο, πρέπει να κάνεις σερί κίνησης {{goal_value}}. Μπορείς να τα καταφέρεις!" and "Το τρέχον σερί σου είναι %#@progress_value@." (iOS 26.1 (23B86) runtime: `System/Library/Health/Assets/FitnessUIAssets.bundle/ActivityAchievements/MonthlyAchievements/localization/challenge/MoveStreak_SMALL_INCREASE` | `Localizable-Modified-tinker.stringsdict` | `ACHIEVEMENT_UNACHIEVED_ALERT_DESCRIPTION/NSStringLocalizedFormatKey`; `…/MoveStreak` | `Localizable-Modified-tinker.stringsdict` | `ACHIEVEMENT_UNACHIEVED_DESCRIPTION/NSStringLocalizedFormatKey`). The same file's default `Localizable.stringsdict` says the same thing in `εσείς`, so the split is a register choice Apple makes per audience, not a rule.
- The app speaks about itself in the first person plural where `en` does (19 `en` keys open "We couldn't…", "We covered…"): "Δεν μπορέσαμε να αποθηκεύσουμε…" (`insights.mood.saveError`), "Καλύψαμε την %@ με ένα πάγωμα σερί." (`streakFreeze.usedBody`).

Neutral strategies Greek offers, in the order to reach for them:

1. **Aorist second person** — no gender: "γύρισες" for `achievements.badge.rehired.earned`; "Συγκεντρώθηκες %lld από 7 μέρες." for `achievements.streak.weekAccessibility`. (`achievements.streak.body` is settled by the idiom row below, "showing up" — see Idioms and figurative language — not by this strategy.)
2. **Nominal sentences** — the predicate agrees with a thing, not the reader: "Επτά μέρες, χωρίς κενό." (`achievements.badge.hired.earned`), "Μία ολόκληρη ώρα, χωρίς διακοπή." (`achievements.badge.wageHour.earned`).
3. **Agreement with the object** — "%lld επιλεγμένες" agrees with "θεματικές", not the reader (`themePicker.manageThemes.selectedCount`); "Η εργασία σου αποθηκεύτηκε και είναι έτοιμη." agrees with "εργασία" (`focusTask.createTask.savedMessage`).
4. **Neuter adverbial forms** for scale labels, as Apple does for State of Mind: "Πολύ ευχάριστα", "Κάπως ευχάριστα" (iOS 26.1 (23B86) runtime: `System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/HealthUI.framework` | `HealthUI-Localizable-Chamomile.strings` | `VALENCE_CLASSIFICATION_VERY_PLEASANT`, `VALENCE_CLASSIFICATION_SLIGHTLY_PLEASANT`) — reusable for `insights.mood.veryPleasant`, `.slightlyPleasant`, `.pleasant`, `.neutral`, `.unpleasant`, `.slightlyUnpleasant`, `.veryUnpleasant`.
5. **Verbal nouns** for badge names, which removes the gendered role noun entirely (Open decision `badge_role_nouns`).

`en` hotspots where a literal Greek rendering forces a gender, with the neutral move:

- **Where no neutral form exists**, the default is the masculine singular, and the key's comment says so. No key needs it today under the moves above; the fallback exists so a translator never invents a slash form.
- **Never** a slash or parenthesised ending. Apple itself ships one ("Διαθέσιμος/η", iOS 26.1 (23B86) runtime: `System/Library/AccessibilityBundles/EventKitUIFramework.axbundle` | `Accessibility.strings` | `invite.status.free`); the skill's rule bans it in every locale (`references/locale-decisions.md` `## Voice choices that need locale review`, Gender in UI copy) and wins (Decided conflicts).
- Grep aid, not a validator: `έτοιμ | σίγουρ | παρών | παρούσ | βέβαι | συγκεντρωμέν | μόνος | μόνη | /η | (η)`. It finds reader-directed predicates and slash forms; it cannot see a gendered noun chosen three words earlier.
- The stored profile gender (`Manifesting/Features/User/Domain/Entities/Gender.swift`) is not a copy input without a product decision.

## Header and label forms

- **Sentence case everywhere**, including screen titles the source Title Cases. Greek does not Title Case running headings; Apple's Greek confirms it ("Όρια εφαρμογών", "Γλώσσα και περιοχή", "Διαχείριση συνδρομών"). Capitals stay on the first word and on Apple's own feature names, which Apple capitalises mid-phrase: "Οθόνη Αφετηρίας", "Κατάσταση Συγκέντρωσης", "Χρόνος επί οθόνης" (the second word stays lowercase in Apple's own value — copy it exactly).
- **Uppercase eyebrows and captions.** The 34 all-caps `en` strings stay all-caps in Greek, written uppercase **in the catalog**, never produced by a runtime uppercase (Identity, engineering flags).
  - **Drop the tonos.** Of 1,500 all-caps Greek values read from the iOS 26.1 (23B86) runtime on 2026-09-16, 3 carry a capital tonos and all three are the same word in three bundles (`ΠΡΟΟΡΙΣΜΌΣ`: `System/Library/Messages/iMessageApps/SafetyMonitorMessages.bundle` | `Localizable.strings` | `DESTINATION`, and the same key in `SafetyMonitor.framework` and `SafetyMonitorUI.framework`). Apple's own badge is `ΔΩΡΕΑΝ ΔΟΚΙΜΗ` from `Δωρεάν δοκιμή` (`System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/AppStoreKit.framework` | `Localizable.strings` | `INTRO_PRICE_OFFER_FREE_TRIAL`), all thirteen letters plain Greek capitals.
  - **Keep, and sometimes add, the diaeresis.** 19 of those 1,500 carry Ϊ or Ϋ, because the vowel pair must stay split once the tonos is gone: `ρολόι` -> `ΡΟΛΟΪ` (`System/Library/BridgeManifests/BridgeManifests.bundle` | `SettingsSearchManifest-com.apple.internal.CompanionReturnToClockSettings.strings` | `RETURN_TO_CLOCK`), `λέιζερ` -> `ΛΕΪΖΕΡ` (`System/Library/Messages/iMessageEffects/CKLasersEffect.bundle` | `InfoPlist.strings` | `AXEffectPickerTitle`). Foundation on macOS 15.7.9 (24G830) does both: `"ρολόι".uppercased(with: Locale(identifier: "el_GR"))` = `ΡΟΛΟΪ`, while the bare `"ρολόι".uppercased()` = `ΡΟΛΌΙ`.
  - Worked pairs, seed 2026-09-16: `insights.mood.mood` MOOD -> ΔΙΑΘΕΣΗ; `insights.summary.dayStreakTitle` DAY STREAK -> ΣΕΡΙ ΗΜΕΡΩΝ; `insights.summary.moneyfestingTitle` EST. TIME VALUE -> ΕΚΤΙΜ. ΑΞΙΑ ΧΡΟΝΟΥ; `widgets.focusRing.title` TODAY'S FOCUS -> ΣΥΓΚΕΝΤΡΩΣΗ ΣΗΜΕΡΑ; `insights.summary.todaySubtitle` TODAY -> ΣΗΜΕΡΑ; `insights.summary.lastThirtyDaysSubtitle` LAST 30 DAYS -> ΤΕΛΕΥΤΑΙΕΣ 30 ΜΕΡΕΣ; `onboarding.hourlyWage.unit` "%@ / HOUR" -> "%@ / ΩΡΑ". Not one carries a tonos.
- **Section headers agree with the listed items.** The Daily Plan headers describe "εργασίες" (feminine plural), so they are feminine plural participles: `plan.dailyPlan.plannedHeader` PLANNED -> ΠΡΟΓΡΑΜΜΑΤΙΣΜΕΝΕΣ, `doneHeader` DONE -> ΟΛΟΚΛΗΡΩΜΕΝΕΣ, `anytimeHeader` ANYTIME -> ΟΠΟΤΕΔΗΠΟΤΕ (an adverb, invariant). A flip of the `task` term changes all three, which is why the term is in the glossary and the headers are not.
- **Fragments with no visible noun** agree with the noun the screen implies, which the packet gives as `agreement_target`. `widgets.streak.copy.frozen.small` "Covered by a freeze." implies η μέρα (feminine singular) -> "Καλυμμένη με πάγωμα.", beside `widgets.streak.copy.frozen.medium` "Χθες καλύφθηκε." A masculine or neuter ending on that fragment is the agreement fault the editor checks.
- **Tab titles are nouns**: Moneyfesting, Πρόγραμμα, Στατιστικά, Προφίλ (`main.tab.*`). The tab bar allows about 70pt each at 10pt with no scaling (`references/surface-budgets.md`, platform-default rows), and Στατιστικά is ten characters; render before accepting it.
- **Toggles and state labels name the state, not the reader**, and agree with the labelled noun: "%lld εφαρμογή σε περιορισμό" / "%lld εφαρμογές σε περιορισμό" (`screenTime.restrictedApps.appsRestricted`), "Σε παύση" (`widgets.startFocus.paused`), "Σε εξέλιξη" (`widgets.startFocus.inProgress`). A status pill about the action, not the user, takes the neuter: "Αποθηκεύτηκε" (`common.editor.saved`).

## Numbers, dates, currency and punctuation

All values from `locale-formats.json` `el` (Foundation/ICU, `el_GR`) unless another source is named.

- **Numbers**: grouping is `.` (U+002E), decimal is `,` (U+002C); `2000` -> `2.000`, `12000` -> `12.000`, `1250.5` -> `1.250,5`; four-digit numbers **are** grouped. A literal number in copy follows this ("2.000 ώρες συγκέντρωσης", `achievements.badge.wageYear.*`, where `en` writes "2,000").
- **Currency**: the app's own `AppCurrency.format` gives `120 €` and `1.250,5 €`, the symbol last, separated by U+00A0 NO-BREAK SPACE; the locale standard with fixed decimals is `120,00 €`. The narrow presentation is what EUR uses (`AppCurrency.swift:33-34`). Never hard-code a currency or a decimal in copy: 0 `en` keys contain a currency name, and the placeholder carries the amount. The compact form is broken in Greek (Identity, engineering flags).
- **Percent**: glued, sign after the number, no space — `40%`, `100%`, `-%lld%%`. Multiplication uses `×` as `en` does (5 `en` keys, e.g. `achievements.category.wages.description`).
- **Dates**: `31 Δεκ 2026` (abbreviated), `31 Δεκεμβρίου 2026` (wide, genitive month), `Τρίτη 12 Μαΐ`, `12 Μαΐου`. The month is genitive in a day-month phrase; never nominative.
- **Time**: 12-hour. `Locale.hourCycle` is `h12 (1-12)`, `DateFormatter` template `j` is `h a`, and `09:41` renders `9:41 πμ`, `23:59` renders `11:59 μμ`, with **U+202F NARROW NO-BREAK SPACE** before πμ/μμ. The four axis-label key sets (`insights.appUsage.axis*`, `profile.appUsage.axis.*`, `screenTimeReport.axis*`) therefore take `12 πμ`, `6 πμ`, `12 μμ`, `6 μμ`, not the 24-hour labels `ro` ships (Decided conflicts).
- **Durations**: widget countdowns stay numeric (`25:00`, `1:45:00`); spoken durations are wide (`25 λεπτά`, `105 λεπτά`).
- **Quotes**: « » (U+00AB, U+00BB). Foundation's alternate pair for `el` is “ ” (U+201C, U+201D); the CLDR 46 summary chart for `el` (fetched 2026-09-16, https://www.unicode.org/cldr/charts/46/summary/el.html) gives « » as the primary pair. Apple's own Greek uses « » around app names and quoted labels: «%@» in the mock alert titles, «Επόμενο», «Ακοή». So `moneyfesting.details.quoteWrapper`, `notifications.contentExtension.quoteDecoration`, the 11 straight-quote `en` keys and every quoted UI label take « »; a straight `"` in a Greek unit is a finding.
- **The Greek question mark is the semicolon, U+003B.** Of 400 English strings ending in `?` with a Greek value in the iOS 26.1 (23B86) runtime, 392 end in U+003B, 5 keep U+003F and none use U+037E GREEK QUESTION MARK (scan 2026-09-16; e.g. `System/Library/CoreServices/SpringBoard.app` | `SpringBoard.strings` | `LOGOUT_TEMPORARY_SESSION_LOGOUT_ALERT_TITLE` "End Session?" -> "Τερματισμός συνεδρίας;"). Every question in the app ends `;`: `quotes.user.deleteTitle`, `themePicker.deleteTheme.title`, `themePicker.deleteThemes.title`, `onboarding.age.title`, `onboarding.personalGoal.title`, `insights.mood.dayQuestion`, `.todayQuestion`. Apple also makes these titles **nominal** ("Αλλαγή της εφαρμογής κλήσεων σε «%@»;"), which is the shape to copy and which also removes the gendered "σίγουρος".
- **The interpunct stays U+00B7 MIDDLE DOT**, never U+0387 GREEK ANO TELEIA. The ano teleia is sentence punctuation (a Greek colon-like mark) and Unicode canonically maps it to U+00B7, so the two are indistinguishable on screen and different in text. The 8 `en` keys with `·` (`widgets.tasks.summary`, `paywall.noCommitment`, the "12mo · %@" template) and the one with `•` keep the source character.
- **Latin homoglyphs.** Greek Α Β Ε Ζ Η Ι Κ Μ Ν Ο Ρ Τ Υ Χ and α ο ν ρ υ χ are visually identical to Latin letters and invisible to a reviewer. One word, one script: a Greek word never contains a Latin letter, and a Latin name (Moneyfesting, Face ID, App Store, widget, Wi-Fi) never contains a Greek one. Apple's own OK button is **Greek** Ο+Κ (U+039F U+039A) (iOS 26.1 (23B86) runtime: `System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/UIKitCore.framework` | `Localizable.strings` | `OK`), which is why the app's three "OK" keys need a decision rather than a guess (Open decision `ok_label_script`). Integration check for every Greek batch: flag any unit that mixes Greek and Latin letters **inside one word**.
- **Plurals**: `el` has exactly two categories, `one` and `other`, both required and both reachable by ordinary integers (ICU 76.1.0.0 / CLDR 46.0.0.0). Only the integer `1` selects `one`; **0 selects `other`**, and so do 2, 3, 11, 20, 21, 100, 101, 120, 1000 and every fraction (0.5, 1.5, 2.5). Exercise **0, 1, 2, 11, 21, 101**. Xcode's own list for `el` agrees (`XCStringsParser.framework` `variationConfigFile.plist`, `PluralRule.ComplementedPluralKeys.el`).
- **Numerals attach directly to the noun**, with no partitive and no linking word: "20 λεπτά", "101 μέρες", "2.000 ώρες". The noun agrees in gender with the counted thing, so `one` is "1 λεπτό" (neuter), "1 ώρα" (feminine), "1 μέρα" (feminine), "1 εργασία" (feminine), "1 εφαρμογή" (feminine), "1 κατηγορία" (feminine). Running text spells the `one` value where `en` does not force the digit ("μία μέρα", "μία φορά").
- **Six keys have plural variations in `ro` but a single `en` unit**, and Greek needs the same split because "%lld μέρες" is wrong at 1: `achievements.streak.body`, `.comebackBody`, `.headline`, `.milestoneBody`, `.nextMilestoneBody`, `.weekAccessibility`.
- `widgets.streak.dayStreakCount` is one `en` form for both categories ("%lld day streak"); Greek needs a **genitive plural**: `one` "σερί μίας μέρας", `other` "σερί %lld μερών". Flag it as a plural addition in the report.

## Length trigger

- **A Greek string longer than 1.45× its `en` length goes on the render list** (`catalog_check.py` C5), not into findings. The ratio is measured from Apple's own translations: 79,664 English/Greek pairs from `el.lproj` across 1,269 iOS 26.1 (23B86) UI bundles, 46,057 unique, placeholders stripped, accessibility bundles and tables excluded. 1.45× is the ratio, rounded to 0.05, that Apple's Greek exceeds as often (30.4%) as Apple's Romanian exceeds 1.35× (31.6%), the statistic closest to the `ro` catalog's own 299 of 1135. A flat 1.35× would flag 42.4% of Apple's Greek and stop discriminating.
- **Greek is the widest of the version-1 languages, and it is widest exactly where the app is tightest.** Median 1.286 overall, p75 1.527, p90 1.846. By English length: 1-10 characters median 1.30, p75 1.75, p90 2.33, with **14.7% over 2.0×**; 11-20 characters p90 1.82; 41-120 characters p90 1.56. The 1-10 band is the controls, the bare units, the eyebrows and the widget copy lines, so a Greek control is the string most likely to double.
- The all-caps subset (1,252 pairs) behaves differently: median 1.143, p75 1.5, p90 2.0, 8.1% over 2.0×. Dropping the tonos costs nothing in width, but the tail is still a doubling, and an eyebrow cannot wrap.
- Slots to render first, because the trigger and the tight rows meet there: the Start Focus action capsule (about 118pt of text, 14pt semibold, floor 0.6), the streak medium run-length title (about 163pt, floor 16/20), the accessory rectangular headline and caption (160 × 72 minus margins, floor 0.8), the badge tile name (2 lines, floor 0.8), the tab bar (about 70pt, **no scaling at all**) and the uppercase eyebrows, which cannot shrink into a second line (`references/surface-budgets.md`).
- Counts never establish fit. Verdicts come from render evidence against each view's own floor and cap (`references/surface-budgets.md`, `references/render-evidence.md`). Shortening cuts words, never facts; accessibility strings are exempt.

## Open decisions

Each item is mirrored in `glossary/el.json` `open` with `id`, `options`, `recommended`, `recommended_reason`, `decided_by: "user"`, `status: "open"` and a note carrying the keys, the rule and the meanwhile. No Greek key exists yet, so "meanwhile" here means what the translator writes in the pilot batch, not a locked value to leave alone.

- **`address_form` — εσύ or εσείς.** Affects every app-owned string, roughly 1,100 keys; the mock alerts and the `onboarding.widgets.howTo.*` mirrors are exempt either way, because they copy Apple. Rule: app-owned copy takes one address for the whole app; strings that mirror an iOS label follow the device. Options: **εσύ**, the singular, which Apple itself uses when it coaches (Fitness "Μπορείς να τα καταφέρεις!", `…/MoveStreak_SMALL_INCREASE` | `Localizable-Modified-tinker.stringsdict` | `ACHIEVEMENT_UNACHIEVED_ALERT_DESCRIPTION/NSStringLocalizedFormatKey`) and which matches the app's "friendly coach, dry warmth" voice and the informal `tu` the owner chose for `ro` (app `CLAUDE.md:43`); **εσείς**, the plural, which is Apple's default across iOS Greek (`platform-terms.json` `register_summary.el`) and which keeps app copy and the mirrored alerts in one register on the two permission screens. Recommended: **εσύ**, because the app coaches a single reader about their own hours and the screens where the device's `εσείς` sits beside it are two mock alerts that are quoting iOS on purpose. Meanwhile: the pilot batch writes `εσύ` throughout and `εσείς` only inside the seven mock-alert keys, the adjudicator lists every key whose register would change, and the disclaimer forms in `## Money frame` are already person-free so a flip does not touch them.
- **`focus_session` — the noun for one focus session.** Affects `moneyfesting.details.*`, `focusTask.*`, `widgets.startFocus.*`, `profile.sessionDetails.*`, `onboarding.welcome.preview.*`; the focus word itself is settled as **συγκέντρωση**, Apple's own Greek for the iOS Focus mode (`Applications/Preferences.app` | `Localizable.strings` | `Focus`), so this decision is only about the session noun. Options: **συνεδρία συγκέντρωσης**, both halves Apple's own Greek (`System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/SensorKitUI.framework/AuthGroups/ECG.bundle` | `InfoPlist.strings` | `Session` = "Συνεδρία"), at the cost of a faintly clinical ring, since συνεδρία is also a therapy session; **σεσιόν συγκέντρωσης**, the everyday loanword, warmer but informal and unattested on iOS; **χρόνος συγκέντρωσης**, no session noun at all, which forces a rewrite of the keys that count sessions (`insights.sessions.*`, `achievements.gauge.sessionMinutes`). Recommended: **συνεδρία συγκέντρωσης**, because it is the only option a reader without English understands at first read and the only one Apple already prints. Meanwhile: the pilot uses the recommended form everywhere, never two forms inside one batch, and the adjudicator lists every key that touched the term; no `terms` row polices it until the owner decides, exactly as `glossary/ro.json` leaves `focus_session` open.
- **`badge_role_nouns` — event nouns or gendered role nouns for the 24 badges.** Keys: `achievements.badge.<id>.name` for `clockedIn`, `backOnShift`, `hired`, `rehired`, `salaried`, `permanent`, `tenure`, `doubleShift`, `fullShift`, `longShift`, plus the category names `achievements.category.{comeback,payroll,shift,wages}.name`. Rule: UI copy is gender-neutral and never slashes endings; the workplace metaphor is a product choice. Options: **event nouns** (Πρόσληψη, Επαναπρόσληψη, Σταθερός μισθός, Μόνιμη θέση, Πρώτη κάρτα, Πίσω στη βάρδια), which keep the workplace conceit while naming the event rather than the reader, so nothing inflects; **gendered role nouns** (Προσληφθείς, Μισθωτός…), which read as the reader's job title and force a masculine default on every badge a woman unlocks. Recommended: **event nouns**, because the metaphor survives intact and the gender problem disappears instead of being defaulted away. Meanwhile: the pilot writes the event nouns, the report lists all 24 names as one decision rather than 24 string findings, and a flip is one follow-up batch.
- **`hourly_rate_term` — ωριαία αμοιβή or τιμή ώρας.** Keys: `onboarding.hourlyWage.*`, `profile.hourlyWage.*`, `currency.onboarding.subtitle`, `screenTimeReport.missingWage`, `achievements.category.wages.description`, `widgets.cash.description`. Rule: the rate is what the reader decides their own time is worth, and no wording may suggest the app pays it (`references/voice-card.md` `## Money frame`). Options: **ωριαία αμοιβή**, the everyday Greek term on invoices and freelance work, matching the source's "hourly wage", but built on αμοιβή, a fee someone pays you; **τιμή ώρας**, literally the price of an hour, which sits naturally beside the hero's "your hours have a price" and cannot be read as income, but which spends the word τιμή that `## Money frame` reserves for the subscription price. Recommended: **ωριαία αμοιβή**, because it is what a Greek reader calls the number they are being asked to type, and the disclaimer under the field ("Δεν είναι πηγή εισοδήματος.") already carries the frame. Meanwhile: the pilot uses ωριαία αμοιβή, never the forbidden "ωριαίος μισθός", and the adjudicator checks every occurrence against the money frame.
- **`ok_label_script` — Latin "OK" or Apple's Greek "ΟΚ".** Keys: `common.ok`, `insights.mood.ok`, `quotes.user.okay`, plus the key literal `OK`. Rule: one script per word, and a string that mirrors an iOS label equals Apple's value. Options: **Latin "OK"** (U+004F U+004B), identical on screen, greppable, and safe for any literal assertion; **Greek "ΟΚ"** (U+039F U+039A), which is what iOS shows on its own alert buttons (`System/Library/PrivateFrameworks/UIKitCore.framework` | `Localizable.strings` | `OK`) and what a Greek screenshot of the system contains. Recommended: **Latin "OK"**, because these three are app-owned buttons, not mirrors, the two forms are indistinguishable to the reader, and a Greek Ο inside a two-letter token is invisible to review while breaking every literal comparison. Meanwhile: the pilot writes Latin "OK" and the integration check flags any unit mixing scripts inside one word; `insights.mood.ok` is a mood-scale value, not a button, so it is a separate question and should follow Apple's State of Mind wording instead of being an "OK" at all.
- **`day_compact_unit` — "%lld η" or "%lld ημ." on the share card.** Keys: `share.duration.daysShort` only, with `share.duration.daysSpelled` as its spoken twin. Rule: the brief's short form is the one a local product team already prints, not a new abbreviation (`references/surface-budgets.md` `## Shortening for fit`). Options: **"%lld η"**, ICU's own narrow day unit for `el` (Foundation on macOS 15.7.9 (24G830): `.components(style: .narrow).locale(el_GR)` on a two-day range gives "2 η"), consistent with the ώ and λ in the same triple; **"%lld ημ."**, the everyday written abbreviation, unambiguous beside a digit. Recommended: **"%lld ημ."**, because a bare η beside a numeral reads as the feminine article ("2 η" looks like the start of a phrase, not a duration), and the share card is a rendered image a stranger reads with no context.
