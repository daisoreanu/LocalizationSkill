## Audience and register

- A friendly coach in everyday Icelandic: short sentences, one idea per line, the ordinary word over the exact one. The reader is a phone user, not a linguist, and knows no English — which matters more here than in any other v1 locale, because the rest of their phone is in English and this app is the only Icelandic surface they see.
- Icelandic consumer apps set the register, since Apple sets none. Landsbankinn's own app pages address the reader as `þú` and give instructions as imperatives: "Þú finnur aðgerðina með því að opna **Stillingar** í appinu, skrunar neðst á síðuna og velur þar **Neyðarlokun**." and "Láttu okkur vita" (https://www.landsbankinn.is/oryggi/stillingar, fetched 2026-09-16). Ísland.is, the government portal, does the same ("Leitaðu á Ísland.is", https://island.is/, fetched 2026-09-16).
- Drop English scaffolding. 14 en keys carry "Please" (en-inventory Counts, case-sensitive); Icelandic UI has no fixed politeness particle, so no `is` string gets "vinsamlegast" — the imperative alone is polite in this register.

## Address and gender

- **`þú` throughout, with no formality choice to make.** Icelandic's V-form (`þér`, þérun) is not in living consumer use; the sources that do address a reader in this market use `þú` and the `-ðu` imperative clitic (Landsbankinn "Láttu okkur vita", "Hafðu fjármálin í hendi þér"; Ísland.is "Leitaðu á Ísland.is", "Vissir þú að…"; both fetched 2026-09-16). This is therefore **not** an Open decision: there is no second register a local product team would plausibly pick. Seed 2026-09-16.
- There is no Apple register to defer to: the register-evidence probes for `is` return "no Icelandic string exists" on every one of the five sampled system strings (`per-locale/is/platform-terms.md` `## Register evidence`, `## Register summary` = `{address: not_found, buttons: not_found, case: not_found, label: not_found}`). Where the `ro` brief says "the mock alerts follow the device's formal plural", this brief has nothing to follow — see Open decision `ios_mirror_language`.
- The app speaks in first person plural in dialogs and failure states, as `en` does ("We couldn't save…", 19 en units in first person plural): "Við gátum ekki vistað…", "Við reynum aftur fljótlega", "Við notum %@". Seed 2026-09-16.
- **Gender is the hard part.** Icelandic adjectives and past participles agree with their subject in gender, number and case, so any predicate aimed at the reader forces a guess: "þú ert tilbúinn" (m.) / "tilbúin" (f.) / "tilbúið" (n.). The neuter is not a generic here — in direct address it reads as a specific, marked choice rather than an unmarked one — so the strategy is to avoid the predicate, not to pick a form.

Neutral strategies, in the order a translator should reach for them:

| Strategy | Icelandic shape | en hotspot keys it covers |
|---|---|---|
| finite verb instead of a predicate adjective | "þú náðir markmiðinu" rather than "þú ert búin/n að ná markmiðinu" | `achievements.gauge.goalReached`, `achievements.badge.fullDayFirst.earned`, `onboarding.dailyFocusGoal.subtitle` ("how focused you are") |

| nominal sentence with no reader in it | "Sjö dagar í röð.", "Heil klukkustund, engin hlé." | `achievements.badge.hired.earned`, `achievements.badge.tenure.earned`, `achievements.category.payroll.description` |

| recast "ready" as a time clause; `tilbúinn/tilbúin` is the single worst offender (7 en "ready" hits) | "þegar þér hentar", "þegar þú vilt" rather than "þegar þú ert tilbúin/n" | `screenTime.createRestrictedList.savedMessage`, `focusTask.createTask.savedMessage`, `profile.quoteCategories.comingSoonFootnote`, `shield.reflection.begin.title`, `streakFreeze.ready`, `streakFreeze.inventoryAccessibility` |

- Where no neutral form exists, the shipped default is the **event noun or the neuter agreeing with the thing**, never a slash form, never a doubled ending, never a parenthesised suffix ("tilbúin(n)"). Icelandic UI copy can be written gender-free throughout by the six strategies above, so a gendered reader predicate in a candidate is an editorial finding, not an accepted default.
- `user.gender.*` (en "Other") takes a noun, not an adjective: "Annað" (neuter, agreeing with "kyn"), never "Annar".
- The stored profile gender (`Manifesting/Features/User/Domain/Entities/Gender.swift`) is not a copy input without a product decision.
- Grep aid, not a validator: `tilbúinn | tilbúin | búinn | búin | einbeittur | einbeitt | ráðinn | ráðin | fastráðinn | fastráðin | kominn | komin | ánægður | ánægð | viss | mættur | mætt | stoltur | stolt`. Every hit is a candidate reader predicate; hits inside a quotation or user content are not findings. `stoltur | stolt` matters most on the Achievements surface, whose register `references/surface-briefs.md` calls "Proud, dry" — exactly where a drafted "Þú ert stoltur/stolt" is most likely to slip in.

## Control forms

**Icelandic buttons take the infinitive (nafnháttur), not the imperative.** Evidence, both fetched 2026-09-16: the Icelandic MediaWiki interface labels its controls "Forskoða", "Sýna breytingar", "Hætta við", "Gefa út síðu" (https://is.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:Sandkassi&action=edit) and its tabs and tools "Lesa", "Breyta", "Leita", "Skrá inn", "Búa til aðgang", "Prenta/flytja út" (https://is.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forsíða); Ísland.is labels its actions "Skoða alla þjónustuflokka", "Stofna fyrirtæki" (https://island.is/). The imperative with `-ðu` belongs to running instruction text, not to the control itself: the same Wikipedia page prints the search placeholder as "Leitaðu í 60.466 greinum". **Do not carry `ro`'s bare imperative into `is`** — "Haltu áfram" on a button reads as an instruction shouted at the reader where "Halda áfram" reads as the control's name.

## Header and label forms

- **Sentence case everywhere in app-owned copy**, including screen titles where `en` uses Title Case: "Set Your Hourly Wage" -> "Stilltu tímagjaldið þitt" (`onboarding.hourlyWage.title`), "Delete Task" -> "Eyða verkefni" (`focusTask.alert.deleteTask.title`). Icelandic capitalises the first word of a sentence and proper names only; the Icelandic Wikipedia interface and Ísland.is both print multi-word labels with one capital ("Nýlegar breytingar", "Handahófsvalin síða", "Skoða alla lífsviðburði"; fetched 2026-09-16). 114 en keys of two to four words are Title Case and 48 of the en title strings are — none of that travels.
- **Uppercase eyebrows keep every accent and every Icelandic letter.** `String.uppercased(with: Locale(identifier: "is_IS"))` on macOS 15.7.9 (Build 24G830), run 2026-09-16, maps `þðæöáéíóúý` to `ÞÐÆÖÁÉÍÓÚÝ` and prints "MÍNÚTUR", "VERÐMÆTI TÍMANS", "LOKIÐ", "ÁVINNINGUR". Icelandic has no special-casing rule of its own: there is no dotted/dotless `i` split (that is Turkish and Azeri), no `ß` (that is German), and no accent stripping (that is Greek's tonos). An eyebrow that loses its accents in a render is a font or a `textCase` bug to file, never a spelling choice. `ð` never begins an Icelandic word, so a leading `Ð` appears only inside an all-caps compound and is correct there.
- 34 en strings are all-caps under the en-inventory rule. The 18 that are eyebrows, stat captions, section headers, unit suffixes or badges keep their case in `is` (`insights.mood.mood` MOOD, `insights.summary.dayStreakTitle` DAY STREAK, `widgets.focusRing.title` TODAY'S FOCUS, `onboarding.hourlyWage.unit` "%@ / HOUR", `plan.dailyPlan.*Header`, `paywall.discountBadge`); the 8 axis labels become 24-hour clock literals instead (Numbers, dates, currency and punctuation) and the OK keys are controls. The uppercase set expands hardest — Length trigger.
- Section headers are nouns or neuter participles agreeing with the listed items, not sentences: PLANNED / DONE / ANYTIME (`plan.dailyPlan.plannedHeader` / `doneHeader` / `anytimeHeader`) -> Á ÁÆTLUN / LOKIÐ / HVENÆR SEM ER; "Recently earned" (`achievements.gallery.recentSection`) -> "Nýlega fengin merki" — the participle agrees with "merki" (neuter plural), not with the reader.
- Tab titles are nouns: Moneyfesting, Áætlun, Tölfræði, Prófíll (`main.tab.*`). "Insights" has no one-word Icelandic equivalent that fits a 70pt tab slot; "Tölfræði" (statistics) is the plain choice, as `ro` picked "Statistici" for the same reason.
- Toggles and state labels name the term, not their own state, and agree with the labelled noun: "%lld app lokað" / "%lld öpp lokuð" (`screenTime.restrictedApps.appsRestricted`), "Hlé á lotu" (`widgets.startFocus.paused`), "Lota í gangi" (`onboarding.welcome.preview.blocking.active`). A fragment with no visible noun agrees with the noun the screen implies: `widgets.streak.copy.frozen.small` and `.frozen.medium` are the agreement pair the editor checks against the packet's `agreement_target`, because the small tile has no visible subject and the medium one does.
- Status pills that describe the action rather than the user take the neuter singular: "Vistað" (`common.editor.saved`), "Læst" (`achievements.badge.locked`).
- Weekday and month names are lower case in Icelandic and arrive formatted, never typed: `31. des. 2026`, `31. desember 2026`, `þriðjudagur, 12. maí` (`locale-formats.json` `dates`, ICU on macOS 15.7.9). A capitalised month in a candidate is a finding.

## Short forms and units

One form per concept; exceptions are named per key. All unit forms are Foundation/ICU output for `is_IS` on macOS 15.7.9 (Build 24G830), run 2026-09-16, except where the row says otherwise.

Two rules govern every row. **Icelandic abbreviations carry a full stop** — `mín.`, `klst.`, `sek.`, `d.` — and dropping it is a finding, not a shortening. **Icelandic puts a space between the number and the unit**: every abbreviated form ICU prints for `is_IS` is spaced ("25 mín.", "1 klst. og 45 mín.", "45 sek.", "1 d."), so unlike `ro`, which glues its widget units by convention, `is` has no glued convention to reach for. A tight `%lldmín.` is therefore a **fit concession a render must authorise**, recorded in that key's comment, never a default the translator picks.

## Numbers, dates, currency and punctuation

All values are Foundation/ICU output for `is_IS` from `locale-formats.json` (ICU 76.1.0.0 / CLDR 46.0.0.0) unless a row cites its own run.

- Runtime values arrive formatted through `AppLanguage.effectiveLocale` (app `CLAUDE.md:42`), so no `is` string hard-codes a decimal, a separator or a currency. Never write "krónur" or "kr." into copy: the currency is the user's own choice and independent of the language, and `AppCurrency.format` prints the ISO code (`120 ISK`, `1.250 ISK`, with U+00A0 between number and code; `AppCurrency.swift:33-36`). A literal number typed into copy uses the full stop for thousands: "2.000 klukkustundir" (`achievements.badge.wageYear.*`).
- Grouping separator U+002E FULL STOP, decimal separator U+002C COMMA, four digits grouped: `2.000`, `12.000`, `1.250,5`, `1.250.000`.
- **Compact money is an engineering finding, not a copy choice.** App: `1,25k ISK`, `1,25m ISK`, `1,25b ISK`. Locale: `1,25 þ. ISK`, `1,25 m. ISK`, and plain numbers `1,25 ma.` for milliards. Report it (Identity); do not invent Icelandic suffixes in copy to compensate.
- Time is 24-hour: `Locale.hourCycle` is `h23`, `DateFormatter` template `j` resolves to `HH`, and 23:59 prints "23:59". The four axis-label keys (`insights.appUsage.axisMidnight` / `axisNoon` / `axisSixAm` / `axisSixPm`, en "12AM"/"12PM"/"06AM"/"06PM", plus their `profile.appUsage.axis.*` and `screenTimeReport.axis*` twins) take "00:00", "12:00", "06:00", "18:00".
- Dates: `31. des. 2026`, `31. desember 2026`, `12. maí`, `þriðjudagur, 12. maí` — day number, full stop, lower-case month.
- Percent is glued with no space, sign after the number: `40%`, `100%`, `-%lld%%` (U+0025 only). Multiplication in `achievements.category.wages.description` takes `×` as en does.
- Quotation marks are „ … “ (U+201E, U+201C), singles ‚ … ‘ (U+201A, U+2018). They wrap quoted UI labels inside a sentence ("Ýttu á „Reyna aftur“", `component.errorView.message`) and the quote widgets (`moneyfesting.details.quoteWrapper`, `notifications.contentExtension.quoteDecoration`, `widgets.quotes.placeholderText`). 11 en keys ship straight quotes; none of them stays straight in `is`.
- Direction is `ltr`. No U+202F appears in any rendered `is_IS` value; U+00A0 appears in all 14 money and compact-money outputs, so a normal space typed in place of one is a structure finding.
- Ranges take an en dash without spaces ("1–3 klst.", "18–24", "5–120 mínútur"); asides take a spaced em dash; ellipsis follows the app's three-period convention (Decided conflicts).
- **Plural categories: `one` and `other`, both required, no optional category.** ICU 76.1.0.0 and Xcode's `variationConfigFile.plist` (`PluralRule.ComplementedPluralKeys.is`) agree exactly: no `icu_only` and no `xcode_only` keyword. CLDR's published rule for `is` is the full two-branch form `one: t = 0 and i % 10 = 1 and i % 100 != 11 or t % 10 = 1 and t % 100 != 11` (`t` = visible fraction digits, not `i`), with examples 1, 21, 31, 41, 51, 61, 71, 81, 101, 1001 for integers and 1.1, 2.1, 3.1… for the decimal branch (https://www.unicode.org/cldr/charts/46/supplemental/language_plural_rules.html, fetched 2026-09-16). Every pluralized key in this catalog uses an integer `%lld` placeholder, so `t` is always 0 and the decimal branch never fires here — but it is part of the published rule and is quoted in full above rather than dropped.
  - **Boundary integers to exercise: 0, 1, 2, 11, 20, 21, 22, 100, 101, 111, 1001, 1000000, 1000001.** The surprises: 11 is `other` while 21 is `one` (same last digit); 0 is `other`; 1000000 is `other` but 1000001 is `one`; every fraction tested (0.5, 1.5, 2.5) is `other`, so 1.5 does not follow 1.
  - The category is grammatically real, not a tag: ICU prints "1 mínúta / 2 mínútur / 11 mínútur / 21 mínúta / 101 mínúta / 1.001 mínúta" and "1 dagur / 2 dagar / 11 dagar / 21 dagur / 101 dagur" (`Measurement.formatted(.measurement(width:.wide).locale(is_IS))` and `DateComponentsFormatter` `.full`, macOS 15.7.9 Build 24G830, run 2026-09-16). A numeral ending in 1 (except 11) takes the **singular** noun — the opposite of what an English-trained draft produces for "21 days".
  - 29 en keys carry plural variations and need both `is` categories. Six more keys are a single en `stringUnit` that `ro` splits on the count (`achievements.streak.body`, `.comebackBody`, `.headline`, `.milestoneBody`, `.nextMilestoneBody`, `.weekAccessibility`); `is` needs the same split for the same reason, since "Röð upp á 21 dagur" and "21 dagar" cannot both be right.
  - Two en keys have `one == other` by design (`widgets.streak.dayStreakCount`, `widgets.streak.copy.atRisk.*`); `is` still fills both categories, because the noun changes even where en's does not.
- **Numerals 1–4 inflect and agree with their noun in gender and case**, so a counted noun cannot be moved between slots without rereading the number: "einn dagur" (m.), "ein mínúta" (f.), "eitt verkefni" (n.); "tveir dagar", "tvær mínútur", "tvö verkefni". Running text spells the small count out where en does ("fimm mínútur" in `widgets.streak.a11y.newHere` and `.lapsed`, which en writes as "five minutes"); numeric labels keep the placeholder. A single `%lld` that must agree with a gendered noun in a case other than the nominative is an engineering constraint to flag, not something to bend — say so in the key's comment and propose the merge, per `references/agent-protocol.md` `## A. Forward translator`.

## Length trigger

- **An `is` string longer than 1.15× its `en` length gets a layout check. This is a stated proxy, not a measurement.** `length-stats.json` for `is` reports `pairs: 0` and `ios_ui: false`: there are no Apple `is`/`en` string pairs to measure, because no selected UI bundle ships `is.lproj`. The proxy is borrowed from the lowest of the three measured Nordic triggers — nb 1.15, da 1.15, sv 1.20 — because the lowest puts the most strings on the render list while Icelandic expansion is unknown. `scripts/catalog_check.py` C5 puts a string over it on the render list, not among findings.
- **Replace it with a measurement as soon as `is` copy exists.** The first batch's integrated candidates are the first real `is`/`en` pairs; recompute the ratio over them and amend this section rather than keeping a borrowed number.
- Agent-authored illustrations of where the expansion will bite, seed 2026-09-16 and not measurements: Done -> Lokið (1.25×), MOOD -> LÍÐAN (1.25×), OK -> Í lagi (3.0×), DAY STREAK -> DAGAR Í RÖÐ (1.1×), Continue -> Halda áfram (1.375×), Settings -> Stillingar (1.25×), Got it -> Ég skil (1.17×), "focus session" -> "einbeitingarlota" (1.23×), EST. Rule: `references/locale-decisions.md` `## Platform terms` — app-owned copy uses Apple's app and feature names as that locale's device shows them; here the device shows English. Options: (1) **English names throughout** ("Screen Time", "Settings", "Photos", "Health", "Home Screen", "Face ID", "App Store"), matching what an `is-IS` iPhone prints in all six probed bundles; (2) **Icelandic common nouns throughout** ("skjátími", "stillingar", "myndir", "heilsa", "heimaskjár"), which reads as one language but names controls the device never labels that way; (3) **split by role** — English where the string points at a control the user must find or tap, Icelandic where it names a concept the user only reads. Evidence per option: (1) `apple-coverage.json` `system_negotiation` 2026-09-16, `is-IS` -> `en` in Preferences, UIKitCore, SpringBoardHome, UserNotificationsServer, FamilyControlsAuthUI and StoreKitUIService; (2) the register sources (Landsbankinn, Ísland.is) keep everything Icelandic and Icelandic readers handle unadapted names like "App Store" and "Face ID" without comment; (3) the `en` source already makes this split itself, writing "Photos" for the app and "photo gallery" for the concept (`share.alert.photoAccessDeniedTitle` against `onboarding.alert.photoAccessDeniedMessage`), and "Home Screen" as a menu label against "your Home Screen" in prose. **Recommended: (3)**, because a pointer only works if it names the control the way the user's own screen does, while a concept noun is read rather than searched for, and the `is-IS,da-DK` chain shows the device language is not even constant across users. Meanwhile: agents follow (3), the Platform terms table records which side each key took, `glossary/is.json` `controls.Settings` stays "Stillingar" so app-owned copy is internally consistent, and the adjudicator lists every key that named an Apple product.
- **Language of the iOS mirrors (`ios_mirror_language`).** Keys: `onboarding.screenTimePermission.alertTitle`, `alertMessage`, `alertDoNotAllow`; `onboarding.notificationPermission.alertTitle`, `alertMessage`, `alertAllow`, `alertDoNotAllow`; the illustrated labels `onboarding.widgets.howTo.editPillLabel`, `doneLabel`, `addWidgetLabel`, `editHomeScreenLabel`, `editPagesLabel`, `editWallpaperLabel`, `customizeLabel`, `shareAppLabel`, `removeAppLabel`, `removeWidgetLabel`, `requireFaceIDLabel`, `searchPlaceholder`, and the steps that quote them, `step2` and `step5`.
