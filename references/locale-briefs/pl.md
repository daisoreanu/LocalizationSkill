## Address and gender

- `ty` throughout app-owned copy: 2sg verbs, pronoun dropped, no "Pan/Pani", no "Proszę". Apple's Polish uses the same address even in system alerts, unlike its Romanian, which switches to the formal plural: "„%@” chce wysyłać **Ci** powiadomienia" (`UserNotificationsServer.framework | Localizable.strings | USER_NOTIFICATION_PERMISSION_ALERT_TITLE`), "danych dotyczących **Twojej** aktywności" (`FamilyControlsAuthenticationUI.app | Localizable.strings | NOTIFICATION_AUTH_REQUEST_MESSAGE_INDIVIDUAL`). So a mock alert and the app's own copy can share one register in Polish, and the `ro` mock-alert conflict does not arise here.
- Apple capitalizes the courtesy pronouns inside that informal address (Ci, Twojej). PWN's advice column recommends the same on websites ("wielka litera … wyraża szacunek", `https://sjp.pwn.pl/poradnia/haslo/Pisownia-zwrotow-grzecznosciowych-na-stronach-internetowych;16681.html`, fetched 2026-09-16) while allowing an author addressing a generic reader to choose, provided the choice is consistent ("albo wielką, albo małą literą", `https://sjp.pwn.pl/poradnia/haslo/zaimki-w-zwrotach-do-czytelnika;12352.html`, fetched 2026-09-16); Apple's own Polish support pages use lowercase. Both runtime citations above are permission-alert bodies, so on their own they justify capitalizing only the 9 mock-alert keys that sit beside a real alert; the other ~168 possessive keys never sit next to one, and the support-page evidence for that non-alert setting is lowercase. Consistency is the only rule agents cannot break: Open decision `courtesy_capitalization`, capitalized meanwhile.
- The app speaks about itself in the first person plural, as `en` does in 19 units: "Nie udało nam się zapisać nastroju." (`insights.mood.saveError`), "Spróbujemy ponownie za chwilę." (`widgets.focusRing.a11y.unavailable`), "Pokryliśmy %@ zamrożeniem serii." (`streakFreeze.usedBody`). Seed 2026-09-16.
- Polish marks gender on past-tense verbs (`-łeś`/`-łaś`), on predicate adjectives and participles (`gotowy`/`gotowa`, `zablokowany`/`zablokowana`) and on role nouns, so UI copy aimed at the reader must avoid all three. Strategies, in the order to reach for them:

| Strategy | Polish form | `en` hotspot it solves |
|---|---|---|
| second-person imperative (no gender) | "Skup się dwa dni z rzędu." | `achievements.badge.dayTwo.criterion`, every CTA |
| present tense (no gender in Polish) | "Znajdujesz czas na to, co ważne." | `achievements.badge.hired.earned` ("You are making time for what matters"), `achievements.badge.rehired.earned` ("you're back" -> "jesteś z powrotem") |
| a nominal sentence with the fact as subject | "Siedem dni bez przerwy.", "Pierwsza sesja za Tobą." | `achievements.badge.hired.earned`, `achievements.badge.clockedIn.earned`, `achievements.streak.body` ("You showed up %lld days in a row." -> "%lld dni z rzędu.") |
| the impersonal past in `-no`/`-to`, which has no gender at all | "Zapisano.", "Nie udało się zapisać." | `common.editor.saved`, the 17 `Could not save …` keys |
| agreement with the object, not the reader | "%lld zaznaczone" agrees with "motywy", not the user | `themePicker.manageThemes.selectedCount`, `screenTime.restrictedApps.appsRestricted` |
| drop the reader from a "ready/sure" predicate | "gdy zechcesz zastosować zmiany" instead of "gdy będziesz gotowy" | `screenTime.createRestrictedList.savedMessage`, `shield.reflection.begin.title` ("Begin before you're ready." -> "Zacznij z tym, co masz.") |

- Role nouns have no neutral Polish form, and the badge set is built on them: `achievements.badge.hired.name` "Hired", `.salaried.name` "Salaried", `.rehired.name` "Rehired", `.permanent.name` "Permanent", `.backOnShift.name` "Back on shift", plus the categories `achievements.category.payroll.name` "Payroll" and `achievements.category.shift.name` "The Shift". The shipped default proposed here is the abstract event noun, which keeps the workplace conceit and names no person: "Zatrudnienie", "Etat", "Ponowne zatrudnienie", "Stałe zatrudnienie", "Powrót na zmianę", "Lista płac", "Zmiana". Open decision `badge_role_nouns`; a masculine default ("Zatrudniony") is the alternative, never a slash form or a doubled ending. Gender neutrality is the only axis tested so far; register is a second one still open — "Zatrudnienie" is also the everyday word for the bureaucratic fact of being on payroll ("zaświadczenie o zatrudnieniu", "umowa o zatrudnieniu"), so the abstract-noun route risks reading as flat HR vocabulary on what is meant to be a celebration screen, a cost no citation here has weighed against the gender win.
- `user.gender.*` are labels for the *option*, not the reader, so they agree with "płeć" (f.): "Kobieta", "Mężczyzna", "Inna", "Wolę nie podawać" (`user.gender.female`, `.male`, `.other`, `.preferNotToSay`). The stored profile gender (`Manifesting/Features/User/Domain/Entities/Gender.swift`) is not a copy input without a product decision.
- Grep aid, not a validator: `łeś | łaś | łem | łam | gotow | pewien | pewna | zatrudnion | zablokowany | zablokowana`.

## Header and label forms

- Sentence case everywhere in app-owned copy, including screen titles where `en` uses Title Case ("Set Your Hourly Wage" -> "Ustaw swoją stawkę godzinową", `onboarding.hourlyWage.title`; 114 `en` keys are Title Case). Polish never Title Cases a multi-word heading, and Apple's Polish agrees throughout ("Czas przed ekranem", "Ekran główny", "Język i region", "Bezpłatny okres próbny").
- Uppercase eyebrows and stat captions stay uppercase when `en` is (34 all-caps `en` strings under the inventory's rule), and every diacritic survives: Apple's own Polish all-caps strings keep ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż ("PRZYKŁADOWE DANE" for SAMPLE DATA, `Messages/iMessageApps/SafetyMonitorMessages.bundle | Localizable.strings | SAMPLE DATA`; "PO STUKNIĘCIU", `BridgeManifests.bundle | SettingsSearchManifest-com.apple.internal.CompanionWakeSettings.strings | ON_TAP`; "USTAWIENIA POWIADOMIEŃ"; "WRÓĆ DO ZEGARA"). Polish has no Turkish dotted/dotless i and no German ß, so the uppercase map is per-character and an author writes Ł for ł, never L. Proposals: MOOD -> NASTRÓJ, DAY STREAK -> DNI Z RZĘDU, "%@ / HOUR" -> "%@ / GODZ.", TASKS DONE -> ZADANIA ZROBIONE, EST. TIME VALUE -> SZAC. WARTOŚĆ CZASU, LAST 30 DAYS -> OSTATNIE 30 DNI, TODAY -> DZIŚ. These expand hardest (Length trigger).
- Section headers are plural participles or nouns agreeing with the listed items: PLANNED -> ZAPLANOWANE, DONE -> ZROBIONE, ANYTIME -> KIEDYKOLWIEK (`plan.dailyPlan.*Header`, all neuter plural or an adverb, agreeing with "zadania"); "Recently earned" -> "Ostatnio zdobyte" (`achievements.gallery.recentSection`).
- Tab titles are nouns: Moneyfesting, Plan, Statystyki, Profil (`main.tab.*`).
- Toggles and state labels take a participle agreeing with the labelled noun, and the participle changes case with the numeral: "%lld aplikacja zablokowana" / "%lld aplikacje zablokowane" / "%lld aplikacji zablokowanych" (`screenTime.restrictedApps.appsRestricted`, one/few/many). A fragment with no visible noun agrees with the noun the screen implies, which is the agreement fault the editor checks against the packet's `agreement_target`: `widgets.streak.copy.frozen.small` "Covered by a freeze." implies the day, not the streak, so "Dzień pokryty zamrożeniem." (m.) beside `widgets.streak.copy.frozen.medium` "Wczorajszy dzień pokryty." — never "Pokryta" (f., agreeing with "seria").
- Status pills that describe the action rather than the user take the impersonal `-no`/`-to` past, which is gender-free and shorter than a participle: "Zapisano" (`common.editor.saved`), "Zapisywanie…" for the in-progress form (`common.editor.saving`).

## Numbers, dates, currency and punctuation

- Runtime values arrive formatted through `AppLanguage.effectiveLocale` (app `CLAUDE.md:42`), so no string hard-codes a currency: the app prints `120 PLN` and `1250,5 PLN` with U+00A0 before the ISO code (`AppCurrency.format` uses the `.isoCode` presentation for PLN), while the locale's own standard is `120,00 zł`. Never write "zł" or "PLN" into copy; the placeholder carries both.
- Decimal separator is the comma (U+002C), grouping separator is a no-break space (U+00A0), and four-digit numbers are not grouped: `2000`, `12 000`, `1 250 000`, `1250,5`. A literal number in copy follows the same rule ("2000 godzin skupienia", `achievements.badge.wageYear.*`).
- Percent is glued, sign after the number, no space: `40%`, `100%`, `-%lld%%`. Multiplication uses × ("Godziny skupienia × Twoja stawka godzinowa", `achievements.category.wages.description`).
- Time is 24-hour (`Locale.hourCycle` h23, `DateFormatter` template `j` -> `HH`): `09:41`, `23:59`, and the chart axis labels 12AM/06AM/12PM/06PM become `00:00`/`06:00`/`12:00`/`18:00` (`insights.appUsage.axis*`, `profile.appUsage.axis.*`, `screenTimeReport.axis*`).
- Dates come from Foundation, never from assembled parts, because the month inflects with the pattern: `31 gru 2026` abbreviated, `31 grudnia 2026` and `12 maja` with the wide month in the genitive, but `wtorek, 12 maj` in the weekday+abbreviated pattern, where ICU leaves the nominative. A copy string that glues a day number to a month name will be wrong in one of those patterns.
- Quote marks are „ ” (U+201E, U+201D), alternates « » (U+00AB, U+00BB); Apple's Polish alerts use them ("„%@” chce wysyłać Ci powiadomienia"). Straight `"` never ships: 11 `en` keys carry one, including `moneyfesting.details.quoteWrapper` `"%@"` -> `„%@”` and every quoted UI label ("Stuknij „Spróbuj ponownie”", `component.errorView.message`).
- Asides take a spaced em dash, the spaces being U+00A0, as Apple's Polish does when it converts an English closed em dash ("achievements—all in one place" -> "osiągnięcia — wszystko w jednym miejscu", `ExtensionKit/Extensions/GameCenterSettingsDeviceExpertExtension.appex | Localizable.strings | WELCOME_TO_GAME_CENTER_ALL_NEW_DESIGN_SUBTITLE`, whose Polish string flanks the em dash with U+00A0 on both sides). A name-qualifier pair — a proper name followed by a qualifying tag, not a clause set off from the sentence around it — takes a spaced en dash instead ("Ikona trwającego odtwarzania, bateria – iPhone", `MobileCoreTypes.bundle | InfoPlist.strings | Battery Ongoing Restore Graphic Icon - iPhone`); numeric ranges take an en dash with no spaces ("5–120 minut", `widgets.startFocus.description`). Ellipsis mirrors the `en` key's own mark (9 keys use three periods, 2 use …).
- One-letter words (i, a, o, u, w, z) take U+00A0 after them so they never end a line, which is Polish typographic practice and Apple's ("a także", "i witryn", "W ruchu").
- Plural categories are `one`, `few` and `many`; `other` is reached by no integer at all (only fractions: 0.5, 1.5, 2.5 tested), but `xcstrings` still requires it, so write `other` identical to the `many` form and say so in the key's comment. Attachment to the counted noun:

| Category | Integers 0–2000 | Case of the noun and its modifiers | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `one` | 1 only | nominative singular | `%lld dzień`, `%lld minuta`, `%lld aplikacja zablokowana` |
| `few` | 2–4, 22–24, 32–34 … 92–94, then 102–104, 122–124 … (for n ≥ 100 only n mod 100 matters) | nominative plural | `%lld dni`, `%lld minuty`, `%lld aplikacje zablokowane` |
| `many` | 0, 5–21, 25–31, 35–41 … 95–101, 105–121 … and every large number (10 000, 1 000 000, 1 000 000 000) | genitive plural, noun **and** adjective or participle | `%lld dni`, `%lld minut`, `%lld aplikacji zablokowanych` |
| `other` | none | fractions only | write it equal to `many` |

- Boundary integers to exercise on every plural key: **0** (`many`, not a zero form), **1**, **2**, **5**, **12** (`many`) against **22** (`few`) — same last digit, different category — **21** (`many`), **25**, **101** (`many`) against **102** (`few`), **120** (`many`), and 1 000 000 (`many`). The 29 `en` plural keys plus the 6 keys where `ro` split a single `en` unit (`achievements.streak.body`, `.comebackBody`, `.headline`, `.milestoneBody`, `.nextMilestoneBody`, `.weekAccessibility`) all need the three-way Polish split.
- Because `many` puts the whole phrase in the genitive plural, a counted noun and its modifier must stay in one key; a prefix or suffix key that has to agree with a number the translator cannot see is an engineering flag, not a wording choice.
- A numeral in a non-nominative slot changes again ("po %lld dniach", "przez %lld minut"), so a sentence that embeds a count is written whole, never assembled from a label plus a number.

## Length trigger

- A Polish string longer than **1.25×** its `en` length gets a layout check; `scripts/catalog_check.py` C5 puts it on the render list, not among findings. The ratio comes from Apple's own Polish translations measured against their English sources on the iOS 26.1 (23B86) runtime: 45 109 unique English/target pairs, unweighted, of which 32.5% exceed 1.25×, the same share of Apple's Romanian that exceeds the 1.35× the `ro` brief uses (31.6%). A flat 1.35× would flag only 22.5% of Apple's Polish and would miss the short-string growth that breaks this app's widgets.
- Polish is less expansive than Romanian against English overall (median 1.143 against ro's 1.227), but the short buckets still grow hardest: 1–10 characters median 1.2, p90 1.833, 37.0% above 1.35×; all-caps 1–10 characters p90 2.0. So the trigger fires mostly on one-word controls and uppercase captions — exactly the eyebrows in Header and label forms and the widget lines in Short forms and units.
- No catalog share exists for `pl`: there is no Polish copy to measure, so the first batch's own render list is the first measurement. Counts never establish fit; verdicts come from render evidence against each view's own floor (`references/surface-budgets.md`, `references/render-evidence.md`). Shortening cuts words, never facts; accessibility strings are exempt.

## Open decisions

- **Save verb (`save_verb`).** Keys: `common.save`, `focusTask.createTask.saveChangesButton`, `quotes.user.saveChanges`, `focusTask.createTask.createButton`, `quotes.user.saveQuote`, `share.action.saveImage`, `common.editor.selectionHint`, plus the 17 `Could not save …` failure messages.
