# Surface briefs

Snapshot 2026-09-15 of the app checkout; every path and line here is checkable with `scripts/check_paths.sh`. Read before writing paywall, welcome, onboarding, permission, widget, share, achievement, notification or shield copy. Character budgets and the shortening order live in `references/surface-budgets.md`, render proof in `references/render-evidence.md`, persuasion limits in `references/voice-card.md`, control forms in the locale brief. A surface's job and feeling decide the register before any word is chosen, because a heading written as a button, or a shield written as a nag, reads as translated even when every word is right.

Contents: Per-surface table · CTA rule · Subscription copy checklist · Shield brief · Hero set and candidate path · Hero worked briefs

## CTA rule

App-owned primary buttons are bare imperatives in the app register wherever the source is imperative (ro "Începe", "Continuă", "Începe proba"); a non-imperative CTA exists where the source is one ("Got it" / "Am înțeles") and stays so. Strings quoting iOS labels mirror the device's own wording for that locale, never a fresh translation, because the user will look for that exact label on screen. A toggle or state label names the term, not its own state (`PaywallCopyTests.swift:70-78`).

## Subscription copy checklist

Stated as parity with the source; App Store Review Guideline 3.1.2 (auto-renewable subscriptions) expects the screen to state what is included, price and term, that it renews automatically and how to cancel, so the locale may move a fact but never lose one.

1. Parity: every commercial fact reachable in `en` stays reachable in the target in some element: what is included, price and period, trial length and that billing starts when it ends unless cancelled, auto-renewal, where to cancel, the restore control. A fact may move from a control to the legal line for fit (precedent: ro `paywall.trialToggle` "Probă gratuită" with `legalText` carrying "%lld zile"), never disappear, and the move is recorded in the shortened key's comment (`trialToggle`'s comment still claims a placeholder; fix it). A fact absent from `en` is a source defect to flag, never content the translator adds.
2. Price: `money()` hard-codes USD (`OnboardingSubscriptionPaywallViewModel+LocalizedStrings.swift:13-15`); when StoreKit lands, preserve `displayPrice` as rendered (`references/project-context.md`). Never convert, round or write a price into prose.
3. Badges: numeric first, one convention per locale, orphan `annualPlanBadge` left alone and reported.
4. Urgency: within `references/voice-card.md` limits; the ladder is in memory only, so no "expires at" or dated deadline.
5. Gate: nothing reading as a trial or price claim ships while `PaywallLadder.hasProducts` is false (`PaywallLadder.swift:32`); ungated source lines to flag, not fix: `heroLineThree` "Start free." (`OnboardingSubscriptionPaywallView.swift:317`) and `paywall.noCommitment` (`:101`).
6. Tests: `trialLengthAppearsInLegalText` and `trialToggleNamesTheTerm` check `en` only (`PaywallCopyTests.swift:63,72`), so a target toggle without the number is untested; propose in the report, never apply, extending both over `AppLanguage.supportedLanguageCodes` with the rule that the toggle may omit the number only when that language's `legalText` contains `String(PaywallLadder.trialDays)`.
7. Social proof: `PaywallSocialProof.swift:18-20` ships no rating and no testimonials; none may appear in any locale.

## Shield brief

- Volume and home: 25 messages x (title, body), five per style, plus per-language close labels, all in Swift (`ShieldMessageCatalog.swift:5-154`, `ShieldPresentationCopy.swift:8-13`). `Localizable.xcstrings` is in neither `ScreenTimeShieldConfigurationExtension` nor `ScreenTimeShieldActionExtension` Resources (pbxproj membership, snapshot 2026-09-15), so "catalog complete" never means "shields localized".
- Language path: `AppLanguage.preferredQuoteLanguageCode` (unsupported system language falls back to `en`, `AppLanguage.swift:52-61`; read at `ShieldConfigurationExtension.swift:27`) with a hard-coded `== "ro"` branch in `ShieldMessage.swift:19-25` and `ShieldPresentationCopy.closeLabel`.
- A third language is an engineering flag naming four touch points: per-language fields or a dictionary in `ShieldMessage`, a `closeLabel` branch, `AppLanguage.supportedLanguageCodes` (`AppLanguage.swift:6`), and `CFBundleLocalizations` in `Manifesting/ScreenTimeShieldConfigurationExtension/Info.plist` (en, ro today). Until then a third locale's shields render English silently.
- Envelope: iOS owns layout and native geometry is unverified (`Docs/Research/SCREEN_SHIELD_RESEARCH/RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md:68`), so match the shipped pairs: title one line of six words or fewer ("A gift to your future self." / "Un dar pentru tine, mai târziu."), body one or two short sentences; nothing can be fit-corrected after the fact.
- Style to feeling: companion = on your side; calm = permission to wait; focused = one small next step; reset = a body or attention cue; regroup = after a slip, no blame. The crying-goat art on `.regroup` carries the guilt cue the owner accepted (`RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md:60,63`), so the words stay kind; this is the one exception to the general no-guilt line.
- Never (derived from the catalog and the research, not policy): the app name, "open the app", "you failed", any detection or mood claim ("we noticed", "you seem"), statistics, attributed quotes, exclamation marks. One next step per body, in the second person, in the local everyday form.
- Preview and evidence: `-widgetDebugGallery shield -shieldPreviewRomanian -shieldPreviewIndex <0-24> -shieldPreviewLargeType [-shieldPreviewWebsite] [-shieldPreviewStyle <style>]` (`ShieldDebugGallery.swift:113-129`; page routing `ManifestingApp.swift:48`) renders `en` and `ro` only and is approximate; readiness needs a device capture at default and AX sizes for app and website variants (`VALIDATION.md:58-64`). Shield titles use the hero candidate path; list AI-revised ids in the report because Swift pairs have no catalog state.

## Hero set and candidate path

The hero set is defined by role, with an explicit member list rather than key patterns, because key patterns over-match (format strings, state labels, orphans). Members (snapshot 2026-09-15): `paywall.onboardingSubscription.heroLineOne/Two/Three`, `paywall.hero.reclaimFormat`, `paywall.benefit.*.title`, `paywall.button.startTrial` and `.subscribe`, `onboarding.welcome.slide.*.eyebrow` and `.headline`, `onboarding.withMoneyfesting.*Line`, `onboarding.widgets.howTo.title`, `streakFreeze.info.title` (its brand line is fixed; candidates vary the second line), `share.screenshot.title`, `notifications.*.body`, and the 25 shield pairs as their own batch. Excluded: brand-name titles (`notifications.*.title`, `onboarding.withMoneyfesting.title`), format-only keys, preview mock values, widget state labels, and the nine unreferenced `paywall.onboardingSubscription.*` keys (`annualPlanBadge`, `annualPlanSubtitle`, `annualPlanTitle`, `choosePlanTitle`, `monthlyPlanTitle`, `pricePerMonth`, `primaryButton`, `trialPitch`, `learnMoreButton`; no Swift call site at snapshot).

Path per hero ID: the coordinator writes a one-sentence frame from the source packet; the translator returns three candidates with distinct angles (source-shaped; what a local product team would write from scratch; the most compact that keeps the promise), none marked preferred, each within budget (same line count as source, carousel headlines six words or fewer, the accent-coloured fragment standing alone, no wrap at the smallest size before the view's own `minimumScaleFactor` engages); the editor's blind packet lists them in shuffled order and the editor ranks for local, credible, clear next action and fits the control with one reason line and no rewriting; the coordinator takes rank 1 unless back-translation shows a frame change; losers stay in the coordinator packet with one-line reasons. Role prompts: `references/agent-protocol.md` `## A. Forward translator` and `## C. Target-language editor`.

## Hero worked briefs

**`paywall.hero.reclaimFormat`** — "Take back up to %@." Site: step `.initial` while the before/after block is visible (`OnboardingSubscriptionPaywallView.swift:311-312`), directly under the chart labelled "Now" / "Your goal" and its caption "Based on the screen time you told us about."; placeholder is `paywall.beforeAfter.hoursPerDayFormat` ("3h a day"). Frame: the hours are a target computed from the user's own answer, never a promise. Invariants: a hedge as strong as "up to" (`OnboardingSubscriptionPaywallViewModel+LocalizedStrings.swift:292`), the duration placeholder with its unit, second person, no income vocabulary (the `PaywallCopyTests.swift:89` ban applies in spirit). Budget: one line at `.largeTitle` bold, `minimumScaleFactor(0.6)`, cap `.accessibility3`, no `lineLimit`; draft against the longest sample ("12h a day") at the smallest width. Illustrative angles for ro: source-shaped "Recuperează până la %@." (shipped); local from scratch "Ia-ți înapoi până la %@."; compact "Până la %@ înapoi la tine." Editor ranks blind; back-translation must return the frame: "get up to 3h a day back" is a match, "save 3h a day" (hedge gone) or "earn 3h" (frame changed) is a reject. Evidence: renders of step `.initial` with and without the before/after block, default and `.accessibility3`. The three-line hero (`heroLineOne/Two/Three`, "Your hours / have a price. / Start free.") takes the same path with the extra budget rule that line 3 stands alone in accent colour (`:317-318`) and reads as the free path, never as "trial", while `hasProducts` is false.

**Shield pair `reflection.begin`** (`.focused`, `ShieldMessageCatalog.swift:90-93`) — "Begin before you're ready." / "One imperfect step can be a good place to start." Frame: permission to start now, imperfectly; feeling: one small next step. "Before you're ready" has no Romanian idiom of the same register, so the shipped title changes the image and keeps the promise: "Începe cu ce ai acum." / "Un pas imperfect poate fi un început bun." Illustrative angles: source-shaped "Începe înainte să fii pregătit." (reads translated); local "Începe cu ce ai acum." (shipped); compact "Începe acum, oricum." Budget: title six words or fewer on one line, body one or two short sentences, no exclamation, no app name, no detection claim. Back-translation of the shipped title returning "start with what you have now" is an acceptable adaptation of the frame, exactly as "plouă cu găleata" returning "it's pouring" is; a return of "get ready first" would be a reversal and a reject.
